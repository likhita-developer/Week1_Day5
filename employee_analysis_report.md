# Employee Data Analysis Report

## 1. Problem
The objective is to clean, analyse and visualise employee data to understand workforce distribution, salary patterns and employee performance.

## 2. Data
The dataset contains 100 employee records and 10 columns, including age, gender, department, city, experience, salary, joining date and performance rating.

## 3. Key Insights
1. Employee ages range from 21 to 58 years.
2. Average employee salary is ₹6,13,815.
3. Average employee experience is 9.05 years.

## 4. Recommendations
1. Review salary distribution across departments to support fair compensation.
2. Use employee experience and performance data to plan employee training.

## 5. Limitations
1. The analysis uses only 100 employee records.
2. The dataset may not represent the entire organisation.
3. Correlation does not necessarily mean causation.
